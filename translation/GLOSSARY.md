## 11. Do NOT translate

- `DPS`, `HP`, `XP` (use `DPS`, `HP`; write `опыт` for XP if it reads better)
- Roman numerals (`I.`, `II.`, `III.`, `IV.`, `V.`)
- `[[n]]` markers, `<color=…>` tags, `\n`
- Numeric values and `%`
